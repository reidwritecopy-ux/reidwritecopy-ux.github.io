MIDAS

A tool that makes short explainer videos and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

MIDAS researches a subject, writes a script, checks the claims in it against the sources, narrates it, draws the diagrams, renders the video and uploads it. It is run by one person, for their own channels, and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

What it publishes

Short documentary explainers, each answering one question about why something is the way it is. Every video states its sources, credits every photograph and its licence, and discloses that the narration is a synthetic voice.

  • Like That For A Reason — why everyday things are the shape they are
  • Lines On The Map — borders, enclaves and time zones
  • A Long Way Up — space and deep time, through the evidence
  • Small Print — the standards and committees nobody reads

How it uses the YouTube API

MIDAS uses the YouTube Data API for one thing: uploading finished videos to channels its operator owns, and setting their title, description, tags and thumbnail. It reads nothing belonging to anyone else and has no users other than its operator.

By using it, its operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of any data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
