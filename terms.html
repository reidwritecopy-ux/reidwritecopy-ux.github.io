What MIDAS is

A program run by one person on their own computer, which makes short explainer videos and publishes them to YouTube channels that person owns. It is not offered to the public, cannot be signed into, and has no other users.

Use of YouTube

MIDAS uses the YouTube Data API. Its operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. MIDAS does not grant anyone else access to YouTube through it.

The videos it publishes

  • Every video names its sources.
  • Every photograph is credited with its author and its licence, and only public-domain or openly licensed material is used.
  • Every video is disclosed as containing synthetic narration.
  • Claims are checked against their sources before publication; anything that cannot be supported is cut rather than softened.

If something published is wrong, it will be corrected or removed. Write to the address below.
